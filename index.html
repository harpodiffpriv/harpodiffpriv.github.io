SoK: A Practical Black-Box Framework for Evaluating Differential Privacy in Cyber-Physical Systems

We present a systematization of knowledge (SoK) and a "black-box" scoring framework, Harpo, that quantitatively evaluates papers in this domain along three dimensions: privacy, utility, and safety. This dashboard provides the technical data and metrics used to establish our findings on the current landscape of CPS privacy research.

Explore Github Repository

Introduction

Privacy has become increasingly important due to the rapid development of autonomous systems (AS) and cyber-physical systems (CPS). These systems are used in critical applications such as industrial control and healthcare, raising concerns about the privacy of sensitive data like task execution times and sensor readings. Differential Privacy (DP) techniques have become contenders to improve both the privacy and security of such systems, yet a comprehensive framework for evaluation is still lacking. Our work seeks to fill this gap by providing a standardized scoring system that considers the unique safety constraints of physical hardware.
